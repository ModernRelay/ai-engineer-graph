2. **The ContradictsPattern edge — precise tension, and the drop-option.** `pat-saaspocalypse`'s brief includes Sequoia's *services is the new software* leg: the next large companies sell **completed work** against outsourced-services budgets. Ganesh argues the inverse for the embedded function specifically: the FDE exists to *discover and generalize product*, and selling its output as delivered work — a go-to-market extension, a customer-success function, "customer-facing software engineers whose job is to make the customer successful" — is the failure mode that produces unsupportable one-off systems (`sig-every-hack-goes-to-prod` is the concrete cost). He is not disputing that embedded delivery is ascendant; he disputes what it is *for* and who owns it, which is a direct contradiction of the services-as-product reading and of the two sibling talks in this batch (Meurer: everything converges on FDE including go-to-market; Rekhi: FDE == product engineering, which he would partially endorse). This is the counter-edge the central brief invited, and it is the only one in the set. **Drop-option:** if you read `pat-saaspocalypse` narrowly as a market/pricing pattern, the contradiction dissolves — in that case hold the signal pattern-less alongside `sig-phoenix-isolation-failure` and let both feed the FDE-pattern decision.
3. **Two pattern-less holds in one file is deliberate.** `sig-phoenix-isolation-failure` is the thesis's founding evidence (product built in isolation from the customer fails on contact with real data) and has no honest home in the current registry; forcing it onto `pat-verification-gap` or `pat-model-not-bottleneck` would misdescribe it. It is the third of this batch's cluster-thesis signals, with `sig-fde-term-collapse` (Sierra) and `sig-decagon-fde-equals-product-eng` (Decagon).
4. **FDE thesis proposal (one sentence, NOT coined — same text in all four files):** *As models industrialize execution, the vendor's scarce work moves inside the customer — embedding engineers (and now FDE-support agents) in the customer's own processes to discover, define and guarantee outcomes — which collapses product engineering, services and go-to-market into a single function and drags pricing from seats toward outcomes.* **This file is the cluster's dissent on the second clause** and should be read alongside the proposal: Ganesh accepts that the work moves inside the customer and that problem definition is the scarce skill, but insists the collapse runs toward *product*, not toward services or go-to-market. If a pattern is coined, its brief should carry that contest rather than assert the merged version; `sig-fde-is-product-not-gtm` would then likely become a `ContradictsPattern` on the new pattern instead of on `pat-saaspocalypse`.

If you want richer typing, resolve it externally before seeding; if you'd rather not carry an unspecified company, the alternative is dropping the node and leaving `exp-vinoo-ganesh` unaffiliated, which loses the "what Kepler is doing" claim in `sig-fde-is-product-not-gtm`.

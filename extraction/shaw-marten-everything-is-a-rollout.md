| `el-harbor` | Harbor | framework | harness | **REVISED.** Open-source agent evaluation and RL environment framework from Laude Institute: (1) a format for specifying agentic environments, (2) a framework for performing rollouts in parallel with any agent, any model, any sandbox, on any task, (3) a registry of popular training and eval environment sets ("three or 400" eval sets). Split models by role — cheap and fast for map (Cursor CLI), high-intelligence for reduce — and pick the sandbox provider to match (Modal, or local orchestration with cloud sandboxing).

| `how-quantize-by-layer-sensitivity` | Leverage the architecture; never quantize uniformly | Uniform/random compression at 86% doesn't make a model 86% dumber, it makes it 100% dumber — exploit structure instead. First and last layers are critical, middle layers compressible; keep linear-attention projections and QKV layers high-precision (quantizing linear attention passes benchmarks then breaks long context); vision/audio layers stay 8/16-bit; sparse MoE layers take aggressive quantization; choose layers via calibration data + gradient-based sensitivity analysis (LP/knapsack solvers automate it); treat selection as combinatorial (layer pairs/triples, per-tensor decisions), and hunt super-weights — one wrongly quantized number can cost 20% of capability.

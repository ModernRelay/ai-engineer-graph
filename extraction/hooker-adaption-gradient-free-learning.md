Its content is participation structure, Auto Scientist, and the death-of-scaling argument. Either the billed title describes the lab's approach rather than this talk, or the talk was substantially reshaped. **No element is coined for gradient-free learning**, and the `ia-` node keeps the billed title for link fidelity while every signal is drawn from delivered content. If the lab's gradient-free method matters to the corpus, it needs a different source.
2. **Where this file sits in the batch.** It is the only talk arguing continual learning matters for **who gets to build**, not how. Its three signals on scaling and compute shape make it the batch's second-strongest contributor to `pat-sovereign-ai` after Druga, and — importantly — it converges with b18's Modal talk from the opposite direction: Jiang argues scattered compute becomes usable for post-training; Hooker argues post-training is where returns now live. Together they are the two halves of the **`pat-compute-liquidity`** candidate proposed in b18. Worth reading side by side at the pattern review.
3. **Signal-bar caveat — this is the batch's weakest evidence base despite the strongest credentials.** The Auto Scientist results are described without a table, benchmark or baseline beyond "outperforms research staff" and "win rates 60+ because we stopped at 60."

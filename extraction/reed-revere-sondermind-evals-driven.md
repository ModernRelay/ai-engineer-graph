| `el-guardrail-sandwich` | Guardrail sandwich (separate-call input/output guardrails) | concept | harness | Safety implemented as independent LLM-as-judge calls that sandwich the core agent rather than as rules inside its prompt. The **input guardrail** inspects the incoming user message and decides whether intervention is required before the core responds; the **output guardrail** inspects the AI response *and the conversation as a whole*, and can intervene to keep the conversation on track. Three stated reasons for keeping them as separate calls: modularity (the core can be iterated without compromising user safety), robustness (separate calls are harder to prompt-engineer around and harder to jailbreak by conversationally driving the model off the rails), and evaluability (modularity makes evaluation straightforward).

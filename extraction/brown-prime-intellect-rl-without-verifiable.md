| `el-signal-manufacturing` | Manufacturing reward signal | concept | training | Umbrella technique set for creating supervision where none exists, all of which spend inference compute to buy it. **Grounding:** take source material and A/B the model with and without it in context — the capability gap between the two runs *is* the signal. **Judges:** LLMs are already strong general reasoners, so a correctly framed judge can spend compute deciding whether an action was good. **Scaling search:** apply test-time search at every layer — mining traces, generating tasks, building worlds, extracting the criteria the judges grade against. | `el-working-backwards-task-synthesis` | Working-backwards task synthesis | concept | training | Build tasks from a known-reachable end state and then throw the solution away. Documents: sample a corpus, have models generate grounded question/answer pairs, verify with other grounded models that the questions are answerable, then discard the source so the real task is upstream of the answer. Code: take real PRs — diffs, descriptions, test cases — and remove pieces of files, so a completed artifact becomes a target you *know* is reachable. General principle: "verify the easy problem and learn on the hard problem"; supervision for free wherever you can move backwards from a solution |
| `el-world-simulator` | World simulator | concept | training | Learned, high-fidelity simulation of the systems you cannot program against — MCP or CLI tools, websites, applications whose back-end state you don't control.

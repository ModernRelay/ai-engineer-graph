| `el-bio-eval-properties` | Three properties of a good biological eval | concept | harness | (1) **Verifiable** — the success condition is checkable by a function. | `el-analysis-dag-decomposition` | Analysis-DAG decomposition | concept | harness | Response to sparse reward while models are weak: grading only the end scientific outcome yields almost no signal, so break the analysis DAG into manageable component steps, take the data to the state it would be in **right before a scientist or theoretician does the interpretive work**, and define ground truth at that node. | `el-choke-point-rubrics` | Choke-point rubrics | concept | harness | For long-horizon science tasks where a terminal verifiable reward is "somewhat uninformative": treat the set of valid analysis paths as a tree, find nodes that are **invariant across paths**, and build rubrics at those choke points using knowledge of how the task works.

| `el-value-model-critic` | Value model / critic for long-horizon RL | technology | training | Reintroducing a learned critic to fix what GRPO cannot handle at long horizons. | `el-pipeline-rl` | Pipeline RL | technology | training | "Quite a popular technique nowadays": instead of running inference to completion and then training, **begin training on the sequences already generated while more are still being generated**. It breaks at long horizons: when a single rollout takes weeks, the run inevitably exceeds that off-policy budget, and the GPUs "just sit there idle and wait for it to finish" unless you bring in a value model and accept its bias instead |
| `el-kelly-bench` | KellyBench | technology | harness | General Reasoning's long-horizon benchmark, built because existing benchmarks do not test open-ended real-world competence.
